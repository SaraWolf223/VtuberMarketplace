## 1. Positioning

**One-liner:** Local marketplace for cheap, nearby finds — no shipping, no fixed price cap.

**Not:** OfferUp clone, nationwide shipping, high-ticket resale.

**Is:** Garage-sale energy, treasure-hunt scrolling, **campus-first but open to everyone** — college students and young adults decluttering dorms and first apartments.

---

## 2. App Store copy (draft)

### Subtitle (30 chars)

`Cheap local pickup deals`

### Description (short)

Local Marketplace is the local way to buy and sell cheap stuff near you — furniture, gaming gear, dorm essentials, and random treasures.

Set your ZIP and radius. Browse what’s actually nearby. Meet up. Done. No shipping headaches.

**Sell in minutes.** Snap photos, set your price, and publish.

**Stay local.** Built for pickup, not packages.

**Optional seller tools.** Boost a listing for more visibility or go Premium for extra perks.

### Keywords

local, marketplace, cheap, garage sale, thrift, dorm, furniture, nearby, deals

## 5. TikTok hooks (scripts)

1. **"POV: everything on this app is actually cheap"`** — scroll feed, react to prices.
2. **"Hidden deals within 10 miles of [campus]"`** — map pin + listing reveal.
3. **"Weirdest thing I found for $8"`** — humor + show item.
4. **"Moving out? List it in 5 minutes"`** — sell flow timelapse.

Hashtags: `#localmarketplace` `#localdeals` `#thrift` `#college` `#[cityname]`

Use **series format:** "Day N of cheap finds in [city]".

---

## 6. Founding Seller badge

- Label: **Founding Seller**
- Who: waitlist signups (auto code on landing) or admin **founding_seller** promo codes
- Perks: badge + **10 free boosts/month** (Founding pool; Premium adds its own 10/mo if subscribed)
- Where: profile header + chip on listing cards in feed
- Redeem: Settings → Redeem promo code (after phone verification; **same email as waitlist** for auto codes)

## 8. Press / one-pager (future)

**Problem:** Big marketplaces are noisy, overpriced, and shipping-heavy for cheap local stuff.

**Solution:** Local Marketplace offers radius discovery and low-price sorting for impulse local deals.

**Traction metric:** Listings per day in one city.

## 9. Voice rules

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Short, upbeat | Corporate jargon |
| Specific (miles, price filters) | Vague "great deals" |
| Honest about meetups | Promise "safe verified buyers" |
