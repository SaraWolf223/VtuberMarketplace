## 1. What a purchase gets you

When you complete a purchase, you receive **exactly what the seller's license grants — nothing more.**

Every listing carries a seller-authored **OTU** (One-Time-Use — full rights/copyright transfer) or **P2U** (Pay-to-Use — non-exclusive usage license) license. The **exact license text shown on the listing at the moment of your purchase** is captured verbatim and stored as an immutable `license_snapshot` tied to your order — this snapshot, not any later edit to the listing, is your permanent record of what you are entitled to do with the asset.

VTuber Marketplace does not author, warrant, or interpret seller license text. If you are unsure what a license permits, ask the seller **before** you buy.

## 2. Accepting the license at checkout

Before you complete checkout, you must check an **acknowledgment box** confirming that you have read and accept the listing's license terms. Checking this box and completing the purchase constitutes your acceptance of that license. The accepted license text is preserved as an **immutable record** tied to your order and remains available to you after purchase, even if the listing or its license text later changes.

## 3. Digital delivery

Assets are delivered via a **seller-hosted download link**, not a file hosted by VTuber Marketplace. After a completed purchase, your order's deliverable includes the seller's `deliverable_url`. VTuber Marketplace does not host, cache, back up, or guarantee the continued availability of that file — see [Terms of Service §7](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md#7-digital-delivery-seller-hosted-links-only).

If your download link does not work, use **Report a broken download link** in-app so we can follow up with the seller and, if applicable, credit a strike under the [Seller Agreement](./SELLER_AGREEMENT.md#5-link-health-and-the-strike-system).

## 4. No-refund policy

**Sales on VTuber Marketplace are final.** Because assets are digital and delivered instantly, we do not offer refunds except in the following cases:

| Exception | What qualifies |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Non-delivery** | You paid but never received a working download link or deliverable |
| **Fraud** | The listing was fraudulent, or payment was made without your authorization |
| **Uncorrectable broken/corrupt file** | The delivered file is broken or corrupted, and the seller cannot fix it within a reasonable window after you report it |

Refund requests outside these categories — including "I changed my mind," dissatisfaction with art style or quality that matches the listing's photos/description, or a compatibility issue not promised by the listing — are not eligible. Contact **support@yourdomain.com** to request a refund under one of the qualifying exceptions above.

## 5. Reviews

You may leave a review for a listing only if you hold a **completed (`fulfilled`) order** for that listing. You may not leave a review for a listing you have not purchased, and you may not manipulate, incentivize, or solicit reviews. See [Community Guidelines](./COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md) for the full review-integrity policy.

## 5A. Model-support subscriptions and work quotes

If a seller offers a monthly **support plan** on a listing you purchased, you may subscribe only after that order is fulfilled. Subscriptions renew monthly until you cancel; cancelling stops future renewals and does not automatically refund the current period. Sellers may send **support work quotes** for extra work beyond the plan; once you accept and pay a quote, that charge is final under the same no-refund rules in [§4](#4-no-refund-policy) (subject to the non-delivery / fraud / uncorrectable-file carve-outs).

## 6. Your responsibilities as a buyer

- Read the listing's license text and photos/description carefully **before** purchasing.
- Use the asset only within the scope of the license you accepted — a P2U license does not grant you the rights an OTU license would, and vice versa.
- Do not share, resell, or redistribute a purchased asset beyond what your accepted license permits.
- Report a broken link, a misrepresented listing, or a seller violation instead of resorting to a chargeback where a qualifying refund request would resolve it faster.
