# VTuber Marketplace Community Guidelines

> **Pre-production draft.** Recommend licensed attorney review before major scaling. Not legal advice.

**Last updated:** [LAST UPDATED DATE — see docs/legal/ENTITY.md]

These Community Guidelines are **incorporated into** our [Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md). They explain what belongs on VTuber Marketplace and what happens when rules are broken.

VTuber Marketplace is a **global marketplace for pre-made digital VTuber assets** — 2D Live2D models, 3D/VRM/FBX models (including VRChat avatars and props), PNGtuber models, accessories, and backgrounds/overlays. It is not a commission/freelance board and not a local/in-person marketplace.

---

## Part A — Listing standards (commerce rules)

*Inspired by marketplace "commerce policies": what a valid listing looks like.*

### Every listing must

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Digital VTuber asset** | A pre-made 2D/3D/PNGtuber model, accessory, or background/overlay — not a commission slot, service, or freelance offer |
| **A license type** | Every listing must be marked **OTU** (One-Time-Use — full rights transfer, delists after sale) or **P2U** (Pay-to-Use — non-exclusive license, stays listed) with seller-authored license text |
| **A working, direct download link** | Your own hosting — no gated surveys, dead links, or unrelated paywalls |
| **At least 3 photos + 1 validated YouTube demo video** | Every listing must include ≥3 photos and exactly one YouTube demo video that passes our embeddability/made-for-kids checks before it can publish |
| **Honest presentation** | Your own renders/screenshots, accurate title/description, honest compatibility and file-format claims |
| **Legal to sell** | You must own or control sufficient rights to sell or license the asset |

### Originality and prohibited-content rules

| Category | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **AI-generated content** | **Banned outright.** Any model, artwork, rig, texture, or accessory generated in whole or material part by generative AI is prohibited. A **confirmed violation escalates straight to an account ban** — there is no warning step for this category, unlike other violations. |
| **Stolen or unauthorized art** | **Banned.** Tracing, ripping, or otherwise using another artist's work without their authorization is prohibited and may also be handled under our [DMCA / Copyright Policy](./DMCA_COPYRIGHT_POLICY.md). |
| **Explicit sexual content** | **Banned outright**, with no age-gate exception. |
| **Mature / suggestive content** | **Allowed if properly flagged.** Suggestive, non-explicit content (for example, swimsuit or lingerie-style outfits) may be listed with the **`is_mature`** flag enabled. Mature listings are age-gated and blurred by default. |
| **Scams & fraud** | Fake giveaways, phishing links, off-platform payment solicitation to bypass Stripe checkout |
| **Duplicate/spam listings** | No posting the same asset repeatedly across many listings to farm visibility |

We may remove content that is **unsafe, deceptive, or harmful** even if not listed above.

### Listing lifecycle

- Listings have **no fixed expiry** — a listing stays live until you unpublish it, it sells under an OTU license, or it is auto-paused by moderation or the link-health/strike system.
- Mark items appropriately and keep your license text, price, and download link current.

## Part B — Licensing responsibility

- **You author your own OTU/P2U license text.** VTuber Marketplace does not provide a mandatory license template and does not warrant or interpret your license terms — see the [Seller Agreement](./SELLER_AGREEMENT.md).
- **You are responsible for your download link's uptime and content.** A broken link that fails our automated health checks, or that is confirmed via a buyer report after a grace period, results in a **strike**. **Three (3) non-expired strikes within a rolling 12-month window result in an automatic account ban.**

## Part C — Conduct and community behavior

*Inspired by platform "community standards": how users treat each other.*

### Do

- Be respectful in messages and reviews
- Report suspicious listings or users
- Use **Block** if someone makes you uncomfortable
- Leave honest reviews after a completed purchase
- Disclose known compatibility limitations (e.g., engine/software version) up front

### Don't

- Harass, threaten, hate speech, or discriminate
- Spam, duplicate junk listings, or keyword stuffing
- Circumvent a ban with new accounts
- Leave a review without a completed (`fulfilled`) order for that listing, or manipulate/incentivize reviews
- Share others' private info without consent
- Use VTuber Marketplace for phishing, pyramid schemes, or off-platform payment traps

## Part D — Messages and contact

- Direct messages are for **listing- or order-related** buyer–seller contact and require a **verified email address** to send.
- No unsolicited marketing blasts.
- We may review messages reported for abuse.

## Part E — Paid features (Pro Seller subscription)

- Pro Seller status (lower, admin-configured commission tier) **does not** exempt you from these Guidelines.
- Misrepresenting your subscription status, or using paid features to evade moderation, violates policy.

## Part F — Enforcement

*Similar to marketplace enforcement ladders: escalating consequences.*

| Stage | What we may do |
|-------|----------------|
| **1 — Reminder** | Remove or edit a listing; send a warning |
| **2 — Restriction** | Pause listings (`paused_pending_review`); limit posting |
| **3 — Suspension** | Temporary ban from posting or messaging |
| **4 — Permanent ban** | Account terminated; evasion accounts banned |

**Exception — AI-generated content:** a confirmed violation skips stages 1–3 entirely and results in an **immediate permanent ban** (see [Part A](#originality-and-prohibited-content-rules)).

**Automated signals:** many reports in a short period, or repeated link-health failures, may auto-pause a listing until a human reviews it.

We are not obligated to provide warnings before action when safety, law, or the AI-content policy requires immediate removal.

---

## Part G — Reporting and appeals

### Report

Tap **Report** on any listing, message, or profile. Include accurate details. False reports may lead to action on your account. To report a broken download link specifically, use **Report a broken download link** on the order.

### Appeal

If you believe we made a mistake, email **support@yourdomain.com** with:

- Your username
- Listing or report ID (if known)
- Why you believe the action was wrong

We will review when possible but do not guarantee reinstatement.

## Part H — Changes

We may update these Guidelines. Continued use of VTuber Marketplace means you follow the current version posted in-app and on our website.
