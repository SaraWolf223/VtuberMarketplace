# Local Marketplace — Seed Content Strategy

**Version:** 1.1  
**Principle:** Supply-first — buyers bounce from empty feeds; sellers join if posting is easy.

---

## 1. Launch principle

**One embarrassingly narrow node, real density, then expand.**

Technical nationwide support ≠ marketing nationwide. If the launch market feels too wide, it is too wide.

---

## 2. City selection criteria

| Factor | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| College population | High |
| Apartment density | High |
| Existing Facebook Marketplace activity | Medium |
| Your local knowledge | High |

**Suggested first cities:** pick one mid-size city + one nearby college town you know well (e.g. a metro area plus a nearby university town). Decide before committing any marketing spend.

---

## 3. Pre-launch seeding (mandatory)

### 3.1 Target inventory (before public launch)

| Metric | Minimum |
|--------|---------|
| Active listings | **40–60** |
| Categories | All 10 with ≥2 each |
| Seller accounts | **25–50** (friends, ambassadors, concierge) |
| Real transaction signal | ≥1 meetup/sale story per 5 active sellers (qualitative OK) |

### 3.2 Concierge onboarding (operator)

- Offer to **post first listing for** new sellers (in person or video call)
- **0% commission** / free boosts for first 30 days in seed city
- **Founding Seller** badge on profile (see **24-Brand_and_Marketing_Copy.md**)

### 3.3 Sources (ethical)

- Your own clutter items
- Friends/family donated items
- Garage sale items you personally photograph and list
- **Do not** scrape/copy others' listings without permission

### 3.4 Listing quality bar

- Real photos (not stock)
- Honest titles
- Varied cheap prices
- Accurate ZIPs in target city

---

## 4. Demand timing (do not skip)

**Do not** run paid buyer acquisition until ALL are true:

- ≥20 active sellers
- ≥50 live listings in target ZIP cluster
- ≥1 real sale/meetup signal per active seller cohort

Until then: 100% effort on supply + content (TikTok, ambassadors, FB groups).

---

## 5. Launch week playbook

### Day -7

- TestFlight beta to **15 local users** (mix buyers + sellers)
- Goal: **+20 listings** from beta sellers

### Day 0

- App Store / Play live (Android first if iOS boost payments are still unresolved — see 10-Monetization_System.md §2)
- TikTok: **3 videos** — series starter: "Day 1 of cheap finds in [city]"
- Post in **2–3** local Facebook buy/sell groups (personal story, not spam)

### Day 1–7

- Daily **"find of the day"** TikTok/Instagram (series format)
- **Every report actioned within 24h** (App Store 1.2)
- DM 3 power sellers; message 3 inactive sellers to relist

### Day 14

- Evaluate listings/day; if **<5/day**, do NOT expand cities — fix seeding

| Offer | Cost |

| Leaderboard | $0 |

**Quota:** **5 listings in week 1** per ambassador (measurable).

---

## 7. TikTok content templates

1. **Series:** "Day N of cheap finds in [city]"
2. **"I found this for $12 on Local Marketplace"** — item + app screen
3. **"Hidden deals near [campus]"** — feed scroll
4. **"Everything here is a steal"** — hook explainer
5. **"Weirdest thing near me"** — humor / treasure hunt

Post **1×/day** week 1, **3×/week** after.

---

## 8. Facebook groups

- Join local buy/sell groups as yourself
- Post once: built a free local app for cheap finds — looking for **sellers** first
- Follow group rules; no daily spam

---

## 9. Expansion gate

Expand to city #2 only after **14 consecutive days** of:

- ≥10 new listings/day
- ≥3 repeat sellers
- Empty feed rate **<30%** of sessions
- Contact rate trending up week-over-week

---

## 10. Failure signals

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Empty feed >50% sessions | Widen radius messaging; more seed listings |
| Listings but no contacts | Better photos/titles; safety/trust copy |
| Reports >2% listings/week | Tighten moderation |
| Downloads but no listings | Seller incentives only |

---

## 11. Operator weekly routine (30 min)

1. Listings/day SQL + supply/demand ratio (see **19-Analytics**)
2. Report queue (<24h SLA)
3. Post 2 TikToks
4. Message 3 inactive sellers — relist or extend
