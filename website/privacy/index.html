3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide and improve the Service (feed, listings, messages, search)
  • Authenticate users and prevent fraud
  • Process boosts and premium payments
  • Enforce Community Guidelines and respond to reports
  • Send transactional SMS (phone verification) and service notifications
  • Comply with law and protect rights, safety, and property
  • Analyze aggregated usage to improve product decisions

    • We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data to run a national classifieds index — discovery stays ZIP + radius bounded.
