FAQ

Questions before launch

What is this marketplace for?

It is for local pickup listings: furniture, gear, books, electronics, household items, and other physical goods that buyers and sellers can exchange nearby.

Does the app handle item payments?

No. Buyers and sellers arrange payment outside the app at meetup. The app may support optional seller tools such as boosts or Premium, but it does not process payment for listed items.

How does the location feed work?

Users set a home ZIP and radius in miles. The feed is filtered to listings inside that local area, with sorting for newest, closest, or lowest price.

What is a Founding Seller code?

It is an early-access promo code generated by the waitlist. After launch, eligible users redeem it in the app to unlock Founding Seller status and monthly boost credits.

Can anyone join, or is it only for students?

The marketplace can be open to everyone. The app also includes an optional college badge flow for buyers who want to identify verified student sellers.

How long do listings stay active?

Standard listings expire after 21 days. Premium listings can stay active for 2 months. Expired listings are permanently deleted after the configured grace period.

How do buyers and sellers stay safe?

Use in-app messages, meet in public, inspect items before paying, avoid deposits or gift cards, and report suspicious listings or users. See the Meetup Safety page before meeting.

When will my area open?

Launch areas can open one ZIP cluster at a time. Join the waitlist so the team can see where buyer and seller demand is strongest.
