FAQ

Questions before launch

What can be sold here?

Pre-made digital VTuber assets: Live2D models, VRM/FBX models (including VRChat avatars and props), PNGtuber models, accessories, backgrounds, and overlays. Physical goods, freelance commissions, and AI-generated content are not allowed.

Does the platform host the asset files?

No. Sellers host their own files and provide download URLs. The platform monitors link health and applies strikes when links break, but never stores or proxies the files.

What are OTU and P2U licenses?

OTU (One-Time-Use) transfers full rights for a single sale and delists the asset afterward. P2U (Pay-to-Use) grants non-exclusive usage rights and the listing can remain available to other buyers. Sellers write their own license text.

How do payments work?

All item sales run through Stripe Connect destination charges. The platform commission is 10% for free-tier sellers and 8% for Pro Seller subscribers (both admin-configurable).

Can I review a listing without buying?

No. Reviews are purchase-verified — you can review a listing only after a completed order for it.

Is mature content allowed?

Suggestive, non-explicit mature content is age-gated and blurred by default. Explicit sexual content and AI-generated content are banned.
