Delete Your Account — Local Marketplace

Last updated: July 11, 2026

You can permanently delete your Local Marketplace account and associated data at any time. Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no recovery period.

Option 1 — Delete from the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Local Marketplace app and sign in
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete account
  4. Confirm — your account is deleted immediately

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, can't sign in, or prefer email, send a deletion request to:

support@yourdomain.com

Send the request from the email address on your account (or tell us your Local Marketplace username/email) so we can verify it's you. We process email deletion requests within a reasonable time, consistent with timelines required by applicable law (for example, within 45 days under CCPA).

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Profile (name, email, photo, ZIP)Deleted immediately
